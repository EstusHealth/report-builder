Manual muscle testing (MRC grades)
Nothing here is pre-filled as normal. Each muscle group starts as not yet tested and you record the grade you actually elicited — the report prints only what you tested, and says plainly what you did not.
▸ Muscle testing reference — MRC grading & technique
MRC / Oxford scale
  • 0 — No contraction palpable.
  • 1 — Flicker or trace of contraction, no joint movement.
  • 2 — Active movement through full range with gravity eliminated.
  • 3 — Active movement through full range against gravity, but not against resistance.
  • 4 — Active movement through full range against gravity and some resistance (4−/4/4+ for mild, moderate, strong resistance).
  • 5 — Normal power: full range against gravity and full resistance.
Technique
  • Position and stabilise the joint proximally so the target muscle is isolated; demonstrate the movement first.
  • Take the limb to end of available range, then apply resistance at the end of range, building gradually — a "break test" — rather than pushing against a moving limb.
  • Test the unaffected side first to establish that client's normal, then compare sides.
  • Grade 4 vs 5 is the common error: if you can break the hold at all, it is a 4, not a 5. Record 4−/4/4+ rather than defaulting to 5.
  • Note pain, effort, fatigue or inconsistency separately from the grade — a grade limited by pain is recorded as such, not as weakness.
  • Do not record a grade for a group you did not physically test. Leave it as not tested and say why.
Why these groups for driving
  • Shoulder/elbow/wrist and grip — steering control, rapid corrective steering, handbrake, gear selection, seatbelt.
  • Hip flexion, knee extension/flexion, ankle dorsiflexion/plantarflexion — pedal transfer speed and sustained braking force.
  • Cervical and trunk rotation — shoulder checks, blind-spot checks, reversing.
